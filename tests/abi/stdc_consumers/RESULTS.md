## Interpretation

- The nine standard headers and their combined C/C++ consumers compile cleanly with the ordinary ISO C spellings and signatures unchanged.
- The checked layer is **additive and opt-in**: `cplus_libc_safe.h` and `stdlib/safe/safe_c.h` are never pulled in by a standard header; their macro redirections are enabled only on explicit inclusion (and can be disabled with `CPLUS_LIBC_SAFE_DISABLE` / `CPLUS_SAFE_C_DISABLE`).
- Any `FAIL` row above is a genuine gap and is reproduced verbatim in the diagnostics section. No header was edited to make a test pass.
- Isolation: `safe_headers_only.cpp` (safeclib-style headers without `cplus_libc.h`) compiles cleanly under C++17, while `cplus_libc_only.cpp` reproduces the exact same diagnostics as both failing overlay consumers. The C++17 blocker is therefore `cplus_libc.h` C-only tokens (`restrict`, `double complex`), **not** missing `extern "C"` and **not** the macro layer of either overlay.
