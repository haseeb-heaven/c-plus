## 1. Standards facts used to interpret the probe

- There is **no published "C21"**. Published ISO C versions are C89/C90, C94 (amendment 1), C99, C11, C17/C18 and **C23 (ISO/IEC 9899:2024)**.
- **C2y/C26 is still a draft**; no published standard exists under that name.
- This clang reports the C23 draft mode as `c2x` / `gnu2x` with `__STDC_VERSION__ == 202000L`. It does **not** provide a `c23`, `c2y`, `c26` or `iso9899:2024` spelling.
- Therefore the only spellings that can actually be tested here are `c2x` and `gnu2x`; the rest are recorded as compiler diagnostics, not as 'unsupported standards'.

## 7. Versions explicitly NOT tested (no compiler flag exists)

- **"C21"** — no such published standard and no `-std` value; nothing to probe.
- **C23 by its published name** — `c23` and `gnu23` are rejected; only the draft spellings `c2x`/`gnu2x` are reachable and they report 202000L, not the final ISO 9899:2024 value.
- **C2y / C26** — draft-only; `c2y`, `c26`, `gnu2y`, `c2y_draft` and `c2b` are all rejected by this compiler.
- **`iso9899:2024`** — no such alias in clang 16 (the `iso9899:` aliases stop at 2018).
- Consequently no C2y/C26-specific language feature can be tested here; any claim about C26 behaviour would be fabricated.
